### Definition

#### Verb

1. To lay as in a bed; to lay in surrounding matter; to bed.
   - *Example: to embed something in clay, mortar, or sand*
2. (by extension) To include in surrounding matter.
   - *Example: We wanted to embed our reporter with the Fifth Infantry Division, but the Army would have none of it.*
3. To encapsulate within another document or data file.
   - *Example: The instructions showed how to embed a chart from the spreadsheet within the wordprocessor document.*

#### Adjective

1. Part of; firmly, or securely surrounded; lodged solidly into; deep-rooted.
2. Partially buried in concrete or planted in earth.
